Policy guardrails

        
ROP = (daily demand × lead time) + safety stock
reorder when (on hand + on order) ≤ ROP

        

          
PR-only — the agent drafts requisitions, never places orders; every PR waits for a human decision.

        

          
MOQ enforcement — quantities below a vendor's minimum are raised to MOQ before the PR is drafted.

        

          
90-day cover cap — blocks overbuying: requested qty must stay within 90 days of demand.

        

          
±15% price sanity — live quotes deviating too far from catalog price are rejected.

        

          
Separation of duties — the requester can never approve their own requisition.

        

          
Rules decide, LLM advises — deterministic policy gates are authoritative; the local model only refines the rationale and quantity.
